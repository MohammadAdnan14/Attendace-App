# Privacy Policy for RBU Attendance Planner

**Last Updated:** October 10, 2026

## 1. Overview
**RBU Attendance Planner** is a free browser extension developed for students of Ramdeobaba University (formerly RCOEM). This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to user privacy and how your data is handled.

## 2. Zero Data Collection
**RBU Attendance Planner does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or user data.**

- **No Remote Servers:** We do not operate external database servers, APIs, or telemetric backends.
- **No Tracking or Analytics:** The extension contains zero tracking pixels, analytics suites (such as Google Analytics), or third-party monitoring scripts.
- **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not collect names, student IDs, roll numbers, credentials, email addresses, or session tokens.

## 3. How the Extension Works
- **Local-Only Processing:** The extension reads attendance records already displayed on the user's student portal (`https://rcoem.in/studentCourseFileNew.htm*`) solely to calculate attendance percentages, bunk margins, and simulate hypothetical attendance numbers in the browser DOM.
- **Client-Side Only:** All mathematical logic and UI state changes execute strictly inside the user's local browser memory and are discarded when the session ends or page reloads.

## 4. Permissions
- **Host Permission (`https://rcoem.in/*`):** Required exclusively to inject the user interface and read course table numbers on the official student portal. No other websites are accessed.
- **Storage:** Used only locally to remember your Dark/Light theme preference and minimized HUD state on your own computer.

## 5. Third-Party Sharing
Because no data is ever collected or transmitted, **no data is ever sold, leased, or transferred to third parties.**
